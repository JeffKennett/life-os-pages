LifeOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

LifeOS is a personal health and fitness analytics application. It connects to WHOOP with the user's explicit authorization in order to analyze fitness, recovery, sleep, and activity data.

Data We Access

Depending on the permissions granted, LifeOS may access:

  • Recovery data
  • Sleep data
  • Physiological cycle and strain data
  • Workout data
  • Body measurement data

How Data Is Used

The data is used only to provide personal fitness, recovery, sleep, nutrition, and training analytics.

Data Storage

LifeOS is currently a personal, local-first application. Health and fitness data is stored locally on the user's device unless the user explicitly enables an external integration.

Data Sharing

LifeOS does not sell personal health information. Data is not shared with third parties unless explicitly authorized by the user as part of an enabled integration.

Authorization

Access to WHOOP data is provided through WHOOP OAuth authorization. Users may revoke authorization through their WHOOP account.
